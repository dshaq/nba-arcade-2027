CARD FORGE

The base set card (Holo Lab, stripped back) and the two ways to grow it. Everything here is a sandbox: tap the buttons to earn EXP and pull duplicates.

HOW IT WORKS
LEVEL UP: a card earns EXP when you use that player (Fantasy starts, Duel picks, Challenge line-ups) or when you feed it a duplicate. Each level unlocks an add-on, and you choose which add-ons are switched on.

EVOLVE: trade in 10 copies of the same base card for his next card, here FIT CHECK, stamped with your name and the date. It starts back at LV 1 with its own, flashier add-ons.

THE CHOICE: every duplicate is either EXP now, or one step toward an evolution.

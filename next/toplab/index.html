TOP 3 LAB

Four ways to show the Weekly Challenge leaders on top of the Challenge page. Each plays its intro the first time you open the page that day. Tap NEXT DAY to see what happens when the standings change overnight. Prizes show as mystery boxes until we decide what's at stake.

2 · HIGH SCORES

The classic arcade leaderboard. Rows type in letter by letter; anyone who moved up flashes NEW! and slides past. Prize boxes sit at the end of each row.

3 · DRAG STRIP

Each leader drives a kart. How far down the track = how many days have gone by, scaled by FP/G against the leader. The finish line is Sunday night, with the prizes waiting at the end.

4 · FIGHTER RANKINGS

A fighting-game select screen. Panels slam in, the champion does a victory hop. A new leader gets a NEW CHAMPION! splash with a flash and screen shake.

6 · THE CLIMB

The week's theme becomes the mountain (WHITEOUT = ice peak, try HEAT for a volcano). Ballers climb to ledges by FP/G, the leader plants a flag at the summit, and the prizes sit on the ledges.

Sample standings for the preview. In the real version these use the live Challenge standings, play once a day per person, and show the top 3 plus your own spot. Prize boxes become the real prizes (packs, card versions, EXP, whatever we decide) once they're set.
